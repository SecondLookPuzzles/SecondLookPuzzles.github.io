About Second Look Puzzles

Second Look Puzzles started with a simple problem: there weren't enough good puzzles to go around.

Its creator, a long-time logic puzzle fan, kept running out of grids worth solving. What could be found online was too thin, too repetitive, or over far too quickly. So they did the obvious thing for a puzzle lover, and started making their own.

One grid led to another. Soon there were whole collections, from gentle warm-ups to proper brain-benders, and keeping them in a drawer felt like a waste. Puzzles are better shared. That's how this site, and the Second Look puzzle books, came to be.

The name fits the hobby: in a good logic puzzle, the next step is always there, waiting for a second look.

What you'll find here

  • Free printable puzzles to download, print and solve with a pencil.
  • Plain-English guides that take you from the rules to confident solving.
  • New free puzzles, added regularly in three difficulties.
  • Puzzle books for when you want a few hundred more.

How the puzzles are made

Every puzzle is checked by computer to confirm it has exactly one solution, so you never need to guess. Each collection runs from easy to hard, so you can start comfortably and build up from there.

Grab a pencil and enjoy. Happy solving!
